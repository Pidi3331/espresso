# Espresso Profiles

Personal espresso profiles for my **Gaggimate Pro**, tuned to my taste.

## Profiles

### [Medium 18g 1:2](profiles/medium-18g-1-2.json)
For medium roasts. Pre-infusion with first-drip bloom, ramps from 9 bar down to 5 bar during extraction.

- 93 °C / 18 g dose / 36 g target / ~25–30 s

### [Adaptive v2](profiles/adaptive-v2.json)
For light to medium roasts with fine grinds. Flow-based pre-infusion, temperature drops to 88 °C for extraction, adaptive flow control.

- 93→88 °C / 38 g target / 25–40 s
